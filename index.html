        Pickup & Delivery
        
          
        We bake in Sacramento and offer pickup and delivery. We do not ship at this time.
        
        
        
        
          
          
        • Pickup: 2801 Arden Way, Sacramento (open map), or another spot you arrange with us, at the date and 1-hour time slot you schedule.
          • 
          
        • We deliver to: Sacramento, Rocklin, Roseville, Placer County, and the Bay Area. We also deliver beyond these areas. Call or text (916) 720-9564 to ask.
          • 
          
        • We will provide a 3-hour delivery window based on the 3-hour window you've chosen when submitting an order.
          • 
          
        • Delivery orders need a $25 minimum (a dozen cookies or brownies, or any cake or pie, qualifies). Single cookies are pickup only.
          • 
          
        • All delivery fees are negotiable for every area and based on distance. Your exact fee is agreed on when we confirm your order.

        Frequently asked questions
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            Do you deliver?
            
        Yes. We deliver to Sacramento, Rocklin, Roseville, Placer County, the Bay Area, and beyond. See Pickup & Delivery for areas, the delivery minimum, and fees.
        
          
        
          
        
            Do you ship?
            
        We do not ship at this time. Pickup and delivery are available.
        
          
        
          
        
            How far ahead should I order?
            
        It depends on the order and the date. Holiday dates fill up fast. Orders under $50 or needed within 72 hours are paid in full. Call or text us at (916) 720-9564 and we'll work it out with you.
        
          
        
          
        
            Do you make custom cakes?
            
        Yes. You can ask for any flavor, design, shape, or change on any item on our menu, including custom cakes. Customization pricing will be discussed when you order.
        
          
        
          
        
            What is your cancellation and refund policy?
            
        See our Order Policy.
        
          
        
          
        
            Do your treats contain allergens?
            
        See the allergen note at the top of our Menu.
        
          
        
          
        
            How long do your treats stay fresh?
            
        Our treats stay fresh up to 3 days.
        
          
        
          
        
            What are your hours?
            
        We're open 8 AM to 8 PM, 7 days a week. Pickup is in 1-hour slots, and delivery is in 3-hour windows. We reply to orders within 24 hours.
        
          
        
        
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        
        
        
          
        Order Policy
        
          
          
            
        • 50% deposit to book your order, refundable only if you cancel 7+ days ahead
          • 
            
        • Orders under $50 or needed within 72 hours are paid in full
          • 
            
        • Balance due 48 hours before pickup or delivery
          • 
            
        • Cancel 7+ days before your order date for a full refund, including the deposit
          • 
            
        • Cancel 3 to 6 days before for a refund of your payments, minus the nonrefundable deposit
          • 
            
        • Cancel 2 days or less before your order date, including the same day or during the delivery window: no refund
          • 
            
        • Cash accepted only after the deposit and delivery fee are paid, unless otherwise negotiated
