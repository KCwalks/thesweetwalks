About SweetWalks

SweetWalks by the Walkers is a family bakery in Sacramento, CA, run by Chance and Kalia Walker. We bake cookies, cupcakes, cakes, brownies, pies, and more with love, made to order and made with family.

Delivery areas

We bake in Sacramento and offer pickup in Sacramento, plus delivery and meetups. We do not ship at this time.

  • Sacramento
  • Rocklin
  • Roseville
  • Placer County
  • Bay Area

We also deliver beyond these areas. You can pick a spot to meet up. Text us at (916) 720-9564 and we will confirm pickup, delivery, or a meetup when we take your order.

We will provide a 3-hour delivery window based on the time slot you've chosen when submitting an order.

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver?

Yes. We are local in Sacramento and deliver to Rocklin, Roseville, Placer County, the Bay Area, and beyond. Pickup in Sacramento and meetups are available too. We will provide a 3-hour delivery window based on the time slot you've chosen when submitting an order.

Do you ship?

We do not ship at this time. Pickup, delivery, and meetups are available.

How far ahead should I order?

It depends on the order and the date. Holiday dates fill up fast. Orders needed within 72 hours are paid in full. Text us at (916) 720-9564 and we'll work it out with you.

Do you make custom cakes?

Yes. You can ask for any flavor, design, shape, or change on any item on our menu, including custom cakes. Customization pricing will be discussed when you order.
