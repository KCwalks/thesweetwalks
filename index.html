Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver?

Yes. We are local in Sacramento and deliver to Rocklin, Roseville, Placer County, the Bay Area, and beyond. Pickup is at 2801 Arden Way, Sacramento, at the date and time you schedule. See Pickup & Delivery.

Do you ship?

We do not ship at this time. Pickup and delivery are available.

How far ahead should I order?

It depends on the order and the date. Holiday dates fill up fast. Orders needed within 72 hours are paid in full. Text us at (916) 720-9564 and we'll work it out with you.

Do you make custom cakes?

Yes. You can ask for any flavor, design, shape, or change on any item on our menu, including custom cakes. Customization pricing will be discussed when you order.

Is there a delivery fee?

Delivery fees start at: Sacramento $5–$10, Roseville $15, Rocklin $20, and the Bay Area $20–$40. All delivery fees are negotiable for every area and based on distance. Your exact fee is agreed on when we confirm your order. See Pickup & Delivery.

Do your treats contain allergens?

Our kitchen handles peanuts, tree nuts, wheat, eggs, and dairy. Products may contain or come in contact with these allergens.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

A 50% deposit books your order. Cancel 7 or more days before your order date for a full refund, including the deposit. Cancel 3 to 6 days before for a refund of your payments, minus the deposit, which is nonrefundable at that point. Cancel 2 days or less before your order date, including the same day or during the delivery window, and there is no refund. See our Order Policy.

Is there a minimum order?

Delivery orders need a $25 minimum (a dozen cookies or brownies, or any cake or pie, qualifies). Single cookies are pickup only.

How long do your treats stay fresh?

Our treats stay fresh up to 3 days.

What are your hours?

We're open 8 AM to 8 PM, 7 days a week. We reply to orders within 24 hours.
